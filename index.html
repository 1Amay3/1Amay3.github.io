Wait, how do I find it? +

Radar is at the bottom center. Straight ahead is 0°; right is positive, left is negative. Convert the echo’s round-trip delay to range: range in meters = 0.15 × delay in nanoseconds. Then use the angle to pick the direction.

Get within 6 m to find the plane. Click or drag, or focus the map and use arrow keys (1 m; Shift for 5 m). Repeated guesses are allowed. The score counts guesses, not skipped planes.

The serious bit: range = c × delay / 2, with c approximated as 300,000,000 m/s. Transmitter and receiver share the radar origin (monostatic). This is an idealized 2D geometry game, not an air-traffic simulation. No noise, multipath, or actual missing planes.
